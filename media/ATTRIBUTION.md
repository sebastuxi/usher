# Demo reel attribution

The Usher demo reel (`reel.mp4`, `reel-9x16.mp4`, their posters and storyboards) is cut from three open movies by the
Blender Foundation, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

- **WING IT!** (2023) © Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org
- **Charge** (2022) © Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org
- **Coffee Run** (2020) © Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org

Changes: excerpts were re-cut into six chapters, centre-cropped to 16:9 and 9:16, colour-preserved, re-encoded, and their
soundtracks crossfaded and loudness-normalised. The chapter titles and the design-notes subtitles are ours.

Sources: https://studio.blender.org/films/ (originals downloaded from the official Blender Studio video archive,
https://video.blender.org).

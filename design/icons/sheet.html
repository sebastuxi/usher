Usher icons

25 icons on an 18×18 grid, drawn for the size they render at. Live area 14×14 (red), circles and triangles overshoot to ~15. Every bar and knock-out 2px on whole pixels; outer corners r1, containers r2, clearance cuts concentric. Solid means now or on, hollow means elsewhere or off.

(a) Actual size 18px icons in 40px buttons — print on a video-like dark, and black on white

(b) 4× vector on a 1px grid with the live area · 1× raster · 2× raster (as the browser actually paints it)

(c, d) Over real frames, as they sit in the player 40px pitch; bright frame gets the shade band (scrim) like the skin

(e) Optical checks 6× vector with centre lines

(v) Variants under review
